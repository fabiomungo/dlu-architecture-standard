# AI-Native University Manifesto

> An AI-Native University is an adaptive academic ecosystem whose purpose is to maximize the lifelong transformation of every learner through the continuous orchestration of knowledge, competencies, artificial intelligence and human mentorship.

## Principles

1. Universities do not merely produce degrees; they transform people.
2. Learners, not courses, are the centre of the system.
3. Credits are administrative units; competencies are educational outcomes.
4. Evidence is required to claim mastery.
5. AI amplifies human education and does not remove academic accountability.
6. Learning continues beyond graduation.
7. Every high-impact automated decision must be explainable, auditable and reviewable.
8. Personalization must remain compatible with academic standards and institutional governance.
