# DXA-600 — Design Tokens & Paper Design 3.0

## File structure
00 Foundations · 01 EKG Primitives · 02 Student · 03 Faculty · 04 Dean · 05 Provost · 06 Cross-role · 07 Prototypes · 08 Handoff.

## Paper rules
- Component names match DXA-200 names.
- Variants map to documented states.
- Frames carry screen IDs (e.g. `STU-01 Student Home`).
- Tokens are semantic (`surface`, `text-muted`, `risk`, `success`, `mastery-track`) rather than one-off colors.
- Mastery, confidence, evidence status and risk use separate encodings.
- Every screen includes annotations for API/view-model mapping at handoff.
