# Adaptive Tutor Roadmap — ATA 1.0

## R0 — Foundation
Student twin, goal model, GraphRAG grounding, tutor session telemetry, expert pedagogical policy.

## R1 — Adaptive MVP
NBLA rules, prerequisite diagnosis, hint ladder, worked examples, learning plan, explain-why, informal TutorEvidence.

## R2 — Evidence-aware Tutor
Qualified micro-assessments, mastery integration, remediation loops, delayed reassessment, faculty visibility.

## R3 — Policy Learning
Propensity logging, offline policy evaluation, controlled contextual-bandit pilot for low-risk interventions.

## R4 — Scale & Optimization
Tenant policy profiles, multi-language evaluation, cost/model routing, advanced retention optimization, institution-level effectiveness analytics.

## Exit criteria
Each release requires learning-effectiveness and safety gates, not only feature completion.
