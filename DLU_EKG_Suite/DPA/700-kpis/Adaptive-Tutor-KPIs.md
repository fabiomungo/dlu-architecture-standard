# Adaptive Tutor KPIs — ATA 1.0

## North-star family
- Learning gain per qualified tutoring hour
- Delayed retention gain
- Goal progress velocity

## Academic effectiveness
- Remediation success rate
- Transfer performance to novel assessment items
- Prerequisite recovery rate
- Mastery calibration error
- Confidence calibration

## Experience
- Recommendation acceptance rate (secondary metric)
- Hint escalation rate
- “Why this?” usage
- Learner-reported usefulness
- Tutor abandonment rate

## Quality & safety
- Grounded response rate
- Policy compliance rate
- Unsupported mastery-update rate (target 0)
- High-risk escalation rate
- Citation/source integrity

## Platform
- P95 turn latency
- Cost per tutoring hour
- GraphRAG cache hit rate
- Fallback rate
- Policy decision latency

Engagement duration must never be used as a standalone optimization objective.
