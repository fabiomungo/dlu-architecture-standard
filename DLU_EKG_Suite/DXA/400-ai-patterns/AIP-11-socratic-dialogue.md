# AIP-11 — Socratic Dialogue

## Purpose
Use guided questions to make the learner reason rather than receiving the answer. Show the current learning goal, allow “give me a hint”, and reveal a direct explanation only after the configured hint ladder or learner request. Never disguise assessment as casual conversation.

## Required states
Default, loading, insufficient evidence, restricted, stale state, accepted, skipped.

## Accessibility
All reasoning and state conveyed visually must have equivalent text. No pedagogical status is encoded by color alone.
