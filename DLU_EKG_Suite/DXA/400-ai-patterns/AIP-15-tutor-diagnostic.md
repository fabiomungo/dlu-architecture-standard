# AIP-15 — Tutor Diagnostic

## Purpose
Use 1–5 targeted questions to reduce uncertainty about a concept. Distinguish diagnostic signals from graded evidence. Show a concise diagnosis: target, likely blocker, confidence, proposed next action.

## Required states
Default, loading, insufficient evidence, restricted, stale state, accepted, skipped.

## Accessibility
All reasoning and state conveyed visually must have equivalent text. No pedagogical status is encoded by color alone.
