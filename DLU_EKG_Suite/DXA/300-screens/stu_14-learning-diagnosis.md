# STU-14 — Learning Diagnosis

## Purpose
Explain a detected learning blocker without overwhelming the student.

## Primary content
- “You are working toward” target.
- Current readiness score.
- Up to three prerequisite blockers.
- Evidence/confidence indicator.
- Recommended remediation path.
- “Review evidence” and “Choose another path”.

## Pattern
Signal -> explanation -> evidence -> local graph. Never open on the graph.
